## Conventions

- Numbers with decimals: speak as "X point Y" (e.g., "six point eight")
- Large numbers: speak naturally (e.g., "two thousand trainers")
- Percentages: "percent" not "per cent"
- "e.g." becomes "for example"
- "i.e." becomes "that is"
- "vs." becomes "versus"
- "etc." becomes "and so on"
