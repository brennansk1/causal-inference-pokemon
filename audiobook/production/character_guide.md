## Character Speech Patterns

### NARRATOR
- First-person perspective of the trainer on the journey
- Warm, curious, engaged -- like a student telling you about their adventure
- Reacts to concepts: "Wait -- so the entire difference could be selection bias?"
- Asks the questions the listener would ask
- Carries all exposition, scene-setting, transitions
- Handles all teaching content that is not character dialogue
- Emotional arc: confusion (Ch1) -> confidence (Ch2) -> humility (Ch3) -> mastery (Ch8)

### PROFESSOR OAK
- Gives plain-English definitions of formal concepts
- Never uses mathematical notation -- describes relationships in words
- Pauses before important points
- Patient with Blue's mistakes, corrects gently but firmly
- Signature phrasing: "Let me be precise about..." / "The key insight is..."
- Ends definitions with concrete examples

### BLUE
- Makes confident causal errors -- says wrong things with certainty
- Primary active learning device: listener should identify the flaw before Oak corrects
- Speeds up when making claims, slows down when being corrected
- Dismissive when challenged: "Details." / "The data speaks for itself."
- Gets progressively more sophisticated in his errors as chapters advance
- Final appearance (Ch8): makes 6 causal fallacies in rapid succession

### GYM LEADERS
- Each appears in their chapter as a problem-poser
- They have the data and the question; they need causal answers
- Brief appearances -- 5-10 lines per chapter maximum
- Their personality matches their type specialty
