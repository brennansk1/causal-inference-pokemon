# Introduction: How to Listen to This Audiobook

NARRATOR: Welcome to Causal Inference: A Pokemon Approach -- Kanto Region Edition. This is an audiobook adaptation of the full textbook, designed from the ground up to be heard, not read. Before we begin, a few words about what you are about to experience and how to get the most out of it.

[pause]

NARRATOR: This audiobook follows a trainer -- you -- on a journey through the Kanto region, from Pallet Town to the Indigo Plateau. Along the way, you will learn causal inference: the science of determining what causes what. Each city presents a new causal challenge, and each Gym Badge represents a new method in your analytical toolkit.

The concepts build on each other. Chapters One and Two lay the foundations -- potential outcomes, randomized experiments. Chapters Three through Five introduce the tools for observational studies -- DAGs, matching, regression, and doubly robust estimation. Chapters Six and Seven handle quasi-experiments -- instrumental variables, regression discontinuity, and difference-in-differences. Chapter Eight is the frontier: mediation, sensitivity analysis, heterogeneous effects, machine learning, and more.

[pause]

NARRATOR: You will meet several recurring characters. Professor Oak is your teacher. He gives clear, precise explanations of every concept, always in plain language. Blue is your rival. He is confident, smart, and consistently wrong about causation. His mistakes are your learning opportunities -- when Blue makes a claim, try to identify the flaw before Professor Oak corrects him. Gym Leaders appear in their respective chapters to pose the causal problems you must solve.

A few tips for listening. First, this audiobook uses deliberate repetition. Every key concept appears at least three times: once informally through the story, once explained by Professor Oak, and once in the Chapter Summary. If something does not click the first time, it will come around again.

Second, you will occasionally hear me say "take a moment to think about this." When you hear that, pause the audio if you can and try to answer the question before the explanation continues. This active engagement makes a real difference in learning.

Third, each chapter ends with a Comprehension Check -- a few questions with answers. Use these to verify that the key ideas stuck.

Fourth, this audiobook covers the same concepts as the written textbook but does not include equations, tables, figures, or code. If you want to see the math, run the analyses yourself, or work through the interactive exercises, the companion Jupyter notebooks are available at the project repository.

[pause]

NARRATOR: One final note. Causal inference is a field built on careful thinking about assumptions. It rewards patience. Do not worry if some concepts feel abstract on first hearing -- they become concrete through the Pokemon examples, and they become second nature through repetition.

Now. Let us begin where every journey begins.

OAK: Welcome to the world of Pokemon -- and the world of causal inference.
